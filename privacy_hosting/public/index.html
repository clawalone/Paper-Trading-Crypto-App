1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Acadrio ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the PaperTrading - Crypto & Stocks mobile application ("App").

By downloading, installing, or accessing PaperTrading - Crypto & Stocks, you acknowledge and agree to the collection and use of data in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum data necessary to operate a secure, interactive trading simulation platform:

  • Account & Profile Data: When registering, we collect your email address, display name / username, avatar choice, and public UID generated via Firebase Authentication.
  • Simulation & Trading Activity: Virtual portfolio balance, open/closed virtual trades, profit/loss (PnL) metrics, tournament rankings, XP milestones, and achievement badges.
  • Device & Technical Diagnostics: Operating system version, device model, language preferences, approximate IP location, crash logs, and app performance analytics for platform stability.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration tokens to deliver voluntary real-time price alerts, trade status notifications, and weekly tournament results.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID) for ad serving and frequency capping, strictly subject to your consent choices.

🔒 No Banking or Financial Credentials Collected

We never request, collect, or store bank account numbers, credit/debit card numbers, private crypto wallet keys, or payment PINs. All financial transactions are strictly prohibited within the simulation.

3. How We Use Information

We process your data exclusively for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Account Management: Authenticating logins, restoring portfolios across devices, and maintaining your virtual paper trading history.
  • Platform Integrity: Calculating real-time weekly tournament standings, XP leaderboards, and preventing automated bot manipulation.
  • Ad Reward Distribution: Verifying completion of voluntary rewarded video ads to grant virtual simulation capital refills.
  • Service Updates: Sending important security notices and critical service announcements.

4. Advertising & Google UMP (GDPR)

We display advertisements provided by Google AdMob to support free access to market simulation data and allow users to earn voluntary virtual cash refills through rewarded video ads.

🇪🇺 European Regulations & Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

For visitors residing in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, we utilize Google's certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) Consent Management Platform (CMP). We collect explicit consent before serving personalized advertisements.

Users have the legal right to accept, reject, or customize consent choices at any time. When consent is denied, only non-personalized, contextual advertisements are served in accordance with Google AdMob policy.

5. Google Play In-App Purchases

PaperTrading - Crypto & Stocks offers optional in-app purchases (such as virtual capital refill bundles and Pro Trader access).

  • All purchases are processed securely and directly by Google Play Billing.
  • We do not receive or store your credit card information, billing address, or payment details. Google Play manages payment processing under its own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
  • All purchased packages grant virtual paper assets only with zero real-world cash equivalence.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with industry-leading infrastructure providers under strict confidentiality agreements:

  • Google Firebase (Alphabet Inc.): Cloud database (Firestore), user authentication, and push notifications. Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob (Alphabet Inc.): Mobile ad delivery, mediation, and rewarded video ads. Google Ads Privacy.
  • Binance Public Market API: Real-time cryptocurrency price tickers and candlestick chart data. No personally identifiable user information is shared.

7. European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or UK, you hold the following rights under GDPR:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal records.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request immediate permanent deletion of your account and trading records.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Revoke ad tracking or data consent choices at any time without penalty.
  • Right to Data Portability: Obtain your virtual profile and trade statistics in a structured format.

8. California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents are entitled to specific disclosures:

  • We do NOT sell your personal information: We have not sold, shared, or rented consumer personal data to any third party for monetary consideration.
  • Right to Know & Delete: You have the right to request disclosure of categories of personal information collected and request deletion.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you in pricing, access, or quality of service for exercising your privacy rights.

9. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your virtual profile information only for as long as your account remains active.

🗑️ How to Permanently Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your account and all associated trading data at any time:

  • Directly inside the app via Profile → Settings → Delete Account.
  • Via our online web portal: Self-Service Account Deletion Page.
  • By emailing us at acadrio.query@gmail.com with your registered email.

Upon confirmation, all Firebase authentication credentials, trading history, and leaderboard records are wiped permanently within 48 hours.

10. Data Security & Protection

We employ multi-layer security protections including TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, strict Firebase Firestore security rules, access token validation, and principle of least privilege access to ensure your information is safeguarded against unauthorized modification or disclosure.

11. Children's Privacy

PaperTrading - Crypto & Stocks is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly delete the data.
